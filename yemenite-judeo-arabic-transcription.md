# Yemenite Judeo-Arabic Transcription

This chart represents Hebrew letters used in Judeo-Arabic according to their
consonant sounds in the Yemenite Hebrew pronunciation convention used here.
Each sound is written with one Latin character. Dagesh is shown where it
changes the pronunciation.

| Hebrew letter | Name | Sound | Latin character |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| א | Alef | glottal stop | ʾ |
| בּ | Bet with dagesh | b | b |
| ב | Bet without dagesh | v | v |
| גּ | Gimel with dagesh | g | g |
| ג | Gimel without dagesh | voiced velar fricative | ɣ |
| דּ | Dalet with dagesh | d | d |
| ד | Dalet without dagesh | “th” in *this* | ð |
| ה | He | h | h |
| ו | Vav | w | w |
| ז | Zayin | z | z |
| ח | Het | voiceless pharyngeal fricative | ḥ |
| ט | Tet | emphatic t | ṭ |
| י | Yod | y | y |
| כּ | Kaf with dagesh | k | k |
| כ | Kaf without dagesh | voiceless velar fricative | x |
| ך | Final Kaf | same as כ | x |
| ל | Lamed | l | l |
| מ | Mem | m | m |
| ם | Final Mem | m | m |
| נ | Nun | n | n |
| ן | Final Nun | n | n |
| ס | Samekh | s | s |
| ע | Ayin | voiced pharyngeal | ʿ |
| פּ | Pe with dagesh | p | p |
| פ | Pe without dagesh | f | f |
| ף | Final Pe | f | f |
| צ | Tsadi | emphatic s | ṣ |
| ץ | Final Tsadi | emphatic s | ṣ |
| ק | Qof | q | q |
| ר | Resh | r | r |
| שׁ | Shin | “sh” sound | š |
| שׂ | Sin | s | s |
| תּ | Tav with dagesh | t | t |
| ת | Tav without dagesh | “th” in *think* | þ |

## Notes

- **þ** represents the unvoiced “th” in *think*; **ð** represents the voiced
  “th” in *this*.
- **ḥ**, **ṭ**, and **ṣ** are single Latin characters with a dot below.
- Final forms use the same Latin character as their corresponding non-final
  forms.
- This chart covers consonants only; vowels are not specified here.
